Trust & safety — how it will work

Trust is the foundation. Here is exactly how verification and safety will work when the community opens — nothing here is live yet, and there are no verification badges in this preview.

Verification (planned)

  • Email verification for every member
  • Mobile number verification
  • Identity check before hosting or guiding
  • Separate host / guide checks before anyone can accept guests

What we will NEVER ask for publicly

  • No Aadhaar numbers in public
  • No passport numbers in public
  • No identity documents posted or shared openly
  • Documents, when needed, go only through a secure verification step — never on a profile

Report & block

Every profile and conversation will carry report and block options. Reported members are reviewed by the community team, and repeat or serious reports lead to removal.

Complaints

A clear complaint path with acknowledgement and a defined response window. Safety complaints are prioritised over everything else.

Emergency support

Members will get India emergency information in one place — police 112, women’s helpline 1091, tourist helpline 1800-11-1363 — plus guidance to contact local authorities first in any real emergency.

Meet smart

First meetings in public places, itineraries shared with family, and no advance payments outside the platform once payments launch.
